The Rooted promise

Clean food. Clean air. Clean ocean. Clean future.

Four promises we keep every day, so that what is good for you is also good for the island.

01 · Clean food
“If it didn’t grow this morning, it isn’t on your plate.”

From our own garden, our own hens and the fishermen next door. Nothing processed, nothing flown in.

02 · Clean air
“Breathe the way the jungle does.”

Car-free grounds, sea breeze instead of noise, and breathwork in the forest at first light.

03 · Clean ocean
“We leave the reef better than we found it.”

Zero single-use plastic, reef-safe sunscreen only, and a reef and beach clean with our guests every week.

04 · Clean future
“Rest well today. Leave a better tomorrow.”

Run on sun and rain. One mangrove planted for every stay, by the children who will inherit it.

Longevity & biohacking

Energy. Recovery. Balance. For the long run.

Longevity isn’t about living forever. It’s about feeling strong, clear and full of energy at 40, 60 and 80. We measure where you start, train what matters and send you home with a plan: rooted in nature, backed by science.

01 · Measure

Know your baseline

You can’t improve what you don’t measure. Day one starts with your numbers.

  • Body composition and grip strength
  • Heart-rate variability and resting pulse
  • Sleep tracking with a smart ring
  • Optional blood panel with a partner clinic
02 · Energy

Train what keeps you young

Muscle, heart and stable blood sugar are the strongest levers there are.

  • Zone 2 cardio on the beach
  • Strength and mobility
  • Muay Thai for power and focus
  • Garden food that keeps energy steady
03 · Recovery

Heat, cold and deep sleep

Progress happens when you recover. We make recovery a daily practice.

  • Cold plunge and wood-fired sauna
  • Red light therapy
  • Thai massage and stretching
  • A sleep protocol for every suite
04 · Balance

A calm nervous system

Stress ages you fastest. We train calm the way we train strength.

  • Breathwork and HRV biofeedback
  • Meditation and yin
  • A digital sunset every evening
  • Coaching on stress and focus

“Add years to your life, and life to your years.”

Rooted is a wellness retreat, not a medical clinic. Optional blood tests are done by a licensed partner clinic and discussed with you by a doctor.

      Good to know

      Questions

      Can't find your answer? Write to our hosts and we'll get back to you within a day.

      Is Rooted a medical clinic?

      No. Rooted is a wellness and longevity retreat. We measure, train and coach; optional blood tests are done by a licensed partner clinic and discussed with you by a doctor. Bring any medical questions to your own physician before you travel.

      Where is Rooted?

      On a Thai island in the Gulf or the Andaman Sea. We will announce the exact location soon. Transfers from the nearest airport are included in every stay.

      Do I have to join every session?

      No. Everything is offered and nothing is required. Your coach helps you choose what fits your goals.

      Can you cater to my diet?

      Yes. Your nutrition intake on arrival sets your menu, and the kitchen handles allergies, vegan and other preferences.

      Are children welcome?

      Children aged 4 and up are welcome during Family weeks, with a full kids' program. Other weeks are best suited to guests 16 and older.

      Is Rooted open all year?

      Yes, 365 days a year. Each season brings its own produce to the garden and its own rhythm to the sea.

      What is your cancellation policy?

      Free cancellation up to 30 days before arrival. Within 30 days we offer a credit towards a future stay.
